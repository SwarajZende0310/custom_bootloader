### Learning continuity

This repository is a stateful teaching workspace. Before continuing the course, read `MISSION.md`, `NOTES.md`, `RESOURCES.md`, and the relevant files under `learning-records/`, `lessons/`, and `reference/`.

Keep the `Current checkpoint` section in `NOTES.md` updated after every meaningful learning session. Record the current lesson, completion evidence, unresolved questions, and exact next action. Record demonstrated understanding separately under `learning-records/`.

Never rely on conversation history, browser-local state, or an untracked file as the only copy of information needed to resume the course. All continuity-critical artifacts must be committed and pushed to this repository.
